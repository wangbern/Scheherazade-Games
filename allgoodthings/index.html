Game Overview

With just your laptop or computer webcam, step into the dance studio and onto the stage.

ALL GOOD THINGS is a digital PC game using the player body as an alt controller.

Play as ANNA, a young dancer who must become a prima ballerina while dealing with challenges as the protege of the extraordinary genius ballet master ROTH.

Technology

We use Unity and Touchdesigner, with body tracking from Mediapipe.


The game does not need any additional hardware to play.

As a game with experimental technology, thank you in advance for your patience as we improve development. Feel free to reach out to scheherazadegames@gmail.com with any questions or bug reports upon release!
